## 1. Purpose
Creator produces and publishes short-form video content promoting SetPoint to their audience. In return, Creator earns a share of the revenue generated by users who join through Creator's tracking code or link. No salary or fixed fee is paid unless stated in section 3.

## 2. Deliverables
- **Minimum content:** [4] videos per month on [Instagram Reels / TikTok], for the first [3] months.
- **Approval:** Company may review a video before it goes live and may request factual corrections about the app. Creative style is Creator's own.
- **Disclosure:** Every post must be clearly marked as a partnership (for example "#ad" / "שיתוף פעולה" or the platform's paid-partnership label).
- **Claims:** Creator will not make medical, weight-loss or health-outcome claims about the app. Company supplies approved talking points.

## 3. Compensation
| Item | Term |
|---|---|
| **Revenue share** | [20]% of Net Revenue from Attributed Users |
| **Net Revenue** | Money Company actually receives from an Attributed User, after app-store fees (Apple / Google), payment processing fees, taxes (VAT) and refunds |
| **Attributed User** | A user who signs up with Creator's unique code or link, or who is recorded as coming from Creator's link |
| **Attribution period** | Each Attributed User counts for [12] months from their first payment |
| **Fixed fee** | [None] (optional: [NIS X] per month, as a minimum guarantee) |
| **Payment** | Monthly, within [30] days after month end, by bank transfer, with a report |
| **Minimum payout** | [NIS 100]; smaller amounts roll to the next month |
| **Reporting** | Company shares a monthly report: signups, paying users, Net Revenue, amount owed. Creator may ask for reasonable backup |

## 4. Tracking
Company provides a unique code or link. Company will keep honest records of attribution. Disputes are resolved by looking at the underlying data in good faith.

## 5. Term and ending the deal
- **Term:** [6] months from signing, renewing automatically for [6] months unless either side gives [30] days' written notice.
- **After ending:** Revenue share keeps being paid on existing Attributed Users until their attribution period ends. No new users are attributed after the end date.
- **Cause:** Either side may end the deal immediately for serious breach (for example, false claims about the app, non-payment, or harmful conduct).

## 6. Content and IP
- **Creator owns** the content they make. Creator grants Company a license to repost and use it in the app's own channels and ads for [12] months after publication. Longer or paid-ad use needs written agreement.
- **Company owns** SetPoint, its name, logo and all app materials. Creator gets a limited license to use them for the content only.
- **Takedown:** Creator will remove a post within [7] days if Company reasonably asks because of a factual error or legal issue.

## 7. Independent status
Creator is an independent contractor. This agreement does not create employment, partnership or agency. Creator is responsible for their own taxes and invoices.

## 8. Exclusivity
- **Category exclusivity:** None, except Creator will not promote a directly competing [nutrition-gap / meal-top-up] app during the term. [Delete if you prefer no exclusivity.]

## 9. Equity (not part of this deal)
No ownership stake is granted by this agreement. If the partnership works, the parties may discuss a small, time-vested stake later. Any equity requires a separate written agreement, a valuation, and legal and tax advice. Nothing here promises equity.

## 10. Confidentiality and conduct
- Each side keeps non-public business information private (app roadmap, numbers).
- Both sides act in good faith and avoid public statements that damage the other.

## 11. General
- **Governing law:** Israel. **Courts:** [Tel Aviv / your district].
- **Changes:** Only in writing, signed by both sides.
- **Full agreement:** This term sheet is replaced by the signed full agreement.

### Notes for you (delete before sending)
- **Percent:** 15-30% is the range I suggested. Start at [20]% and leave room to move.
- **Net Revenue:** App stores keep 15-30% before you receive anything, so defining "Net" clearly protects you from overpaying.
- **Check your real revenue model** (subscription, one-time, free) and adjust section 3 so it matches how SetPoint actually earns.
- **Lawyer review** is most important for: equity (section 9), tax and invoicing, and IP licensing (section 6).
